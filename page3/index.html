About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Michigan, advised by H. V. Jagadish.

My research focuses on data management: helping people discover relevant data, match schemas across data sources, and understand the robustness of rankings. I am also interested in using large language models to support data discovery and integration.

Previously, I received my B.S. in Computer Science with Honors Mathematics from the University of Michigan and worked with Paramveer S. Dhillon on computational social science.
